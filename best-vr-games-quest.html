Best VR Games on Meta Quest (2026)

The best VR games on Meta Quest in 2026 — the essentials every Quest owner should try, across rhythm, adventure, horror, sport, and shooting. All of these run natively on the headset, no PC required.

1. Beat Saber — the game that sells headsets

Slice blocks to the beat with red and blue sabers. Beat Saber is still the most-played VR game on Quest, the safest first purchase, and the benchmark every rhythm game is measured against. If you own a Quest and one game, it is usually this one.

2. Asgard's Wrath 2 — the biggest adventure on Quest

A full-scale action RPG built for standalone Quest: dozens of hours, god-scale combat, puzzles, and companions. The most ambitious game on the headset, and the best answer to anyone who says Quest games are all short experiences.

3. Resident Evil 4 VR — the best horror game on Quest

The full Resident Evil 4 campaign rebuilt for first-person VR, with physical aiming and reloading. A complete, terrifying game rather than a tech demo — and the high point of horror on the platform.

4. The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners — survival horror with weight

A scavenge-and-survive campaign in flooded New Orleans, where every melee swing feels physical and every bullet counts. Deep, grim, and one of the best-written VR games full stop.

5. Walkabout Mini Golf — the game you play with everyone

Miniature golf in impossible, beautiful courses — and the most social game on Quest. Easy to learn, hard to put down, and the best way to spend an evening with friends in VR. Every Quest owner ends up here eventually.

6. Pistol Whip — rhythm action

Shoot enemies to the beat while flying through a cinematic action scene. Half rhythm game, half John Wick — the most stylish shooter on Quest, and the best workout in disguise.

7. Superhot VR — time moves when you move

The most original shooter in VR: time only moves when you do. Dodge bullets in slow motion, grab guns mid-air, and feel like an action hero. Short, sharp, and endlessly replayable.

8. Synth Riders — the dance alternative

Flow through glowing rails and dance to the music instead of cutting it. Synth Riders is the graceful, movement-first rhythm game — the best alternative when you want rhythm without combat. See our best VR rhythm games on Quest round-up for the full genre.

9. Population: One — battle royale in VR

Climb, fly, and fight in a proper VR battle royale. The most complete multiplayer shooter on Quest, and still the best way to play with a squad in VR.

10. Neon Katana — the katana-slicing rhythm game

Our own entry: a VR rhythm game where dual glowing katanas replace the sabers, with an iaijutsu draw-slash mechanic, neon ronin parry-and-counter duels, and a D-to-SSS ranking ladder on every track. It is the newest slicing game on this list, built natively for Quest 2 and Quest 3 — for players who love Beat Saber and want fresh mechanics on top of the same joy.

11. Spirit Sweep VR — ghost hunting on Quest

Our second game: a first-person ghost-hunting horror game where you wield the Spirit Sweeper to banish 16 undead spirit types across 3 haunted zones and 10 missions. The ghost-cleaning entry for Quest players who like Phasmophobia — see our games like Phasmophobia on Quest guide for the full comparison.

12. Moss: Book II — the most charming adventure in VR

Guide Quill, a tiny mouse knight, through a storybook world you can reach into and touch. Moss: Book II is the most charming game on Quest — a reminder that VR's best moments are sometimes the quietest ones.

How we picked this list

Every game here is a real, established Quest title with a lasting player base — no filler, no clones. The order reflects a mix of quality, longevity, and how often Quest players actually come back to each one. Newer games like Neon Katana and Spirit Sweep VR sit further down the list because they are new, not because they are lesser — they earned their places by being original, native Quest games with real mechanics.

What is the best VR game on Meta Quest?

Beat Saber is the most-played VR game on Quest and the safest first purchase. For a full adventure, Asgard's Wrath 2 is the biggest game on the headset. The honest answer depends on taste — rhythm, horror, sport, and shooting all have a standout on Quest.

What are the best Quest 3 games in 2026?

The best Quest 3 games in 2026 include Beat Saber, Asgard's Wrath 2, Resident Evil 4 VR, The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners, Walkabout Mini Golf, and Pistol Whip — all running natively on the headset with no PC required.

What is the best VR game for beginners?

Beat Saber or Walkabout Mini Golf. Beat Saber teaches VR movement in minutes with zero motion sickness for most players, and Walkabout Mini Golf is the most relaxing way to learn the controllers — and the best game to play with friends.
