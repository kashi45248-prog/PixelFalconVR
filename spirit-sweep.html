The estate needs sweeping

Dark dimensional rifts have torn through a decayed Victorian estate, awakening restless wraiths, skeletal reapers, and undead entities that stalk the shadows. Armed with the Spirit Sweeper, you step into the darkness, track down every entity, and banish them before your protective barrier shatters.

One tool, four moves

Everything runs through the Sweeper: ping hidden spirits through walls with your proximity scanner pulse, interrupt incoming attacks with a stun burst, weaken entities with your dual-helix exorcism beam, and pull them into your containment vortex before they break free. The loop is fast to learn and hard to put down — scan, stun, weaken, contain.

Three zones, one haunting

The estate is bigger than one house. Traverse the Victorian Ground Manor, descend into the Blood Catacombs Basement, then step through glowing dimensional portals to the West Octagonal Sanctum Cathedral across the grounds — complete with an Infected Nursery, Crimson Ossuary, Mirror Wing, and Observatory. Doors creak open, chandeliers sway, and periodic 15-second darkness waves plunge the halls into deep shadow as spirits close in.

The finale is a hunt, not a fight

Mission 10 sends you through the portal network to locate and cleanse 3 cursed keepsakes — the Cursed Book in the Manor Library, the Cursed Doll in the West Sanctum Nursery, and the Cursed Candle in the Catacomb Chapel. Only then does the Boss Housekeeper lose her immunity. Find them first, or the finale stays out of reach.

Built for comfort

Play seated or standing with automatic eye-height calibration, smooth thumbstick locomotion, snap turning, an optional comfort vignette, and a left/right-handed weapon toggle — performance optimized for standalone VR headsets.
