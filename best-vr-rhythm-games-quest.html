Best VR Rhythm Games on Quest (2026)

Rhythm is VR's strongest genre — the headset turns music into something you move through. These are the best VR rhythm games on Quest in 2026, from slicing and shooting to drumming and dancing.

1. Beat Saber — the benchmark

Slice blocks to the beat with red and blue sabers. Beat Saber defined the genre, has the biggest licensed track library in VR, and is still the rhythm game every other one is measured against. If you play one rhythm game on Quest, it is this one.

2. Synth Riders — dance first

Synth Riders trades cutting for flowing: ride glowing rails with your hands, catch orbs, and dance through the track. The most graceful rhythm game on Quest, with a freestyle mode that rewards moving your whole body instead of just your wrists.

3. Pistol Whip — rhythm shooting

Shoot enemies to the beat while flying through a cinematic action scene. Pistol Whip is the rhythm game for players who want adrenaline: part music game, part action movie, and one of the best workouts on the headset.

4. Neon Katana — katana slicing

Neon Katana is the katana-slicing entry in the genre: dual glowing katanas, red and blue target matching, and a D-to-SSS ranking ladder on every track. Its own mechanics set it apart — an iaijutsu draw-slash where your katana rests sheathed until the beat tells you to draw, and parry-and-counter duels against neon ronin between tracks. A 2026 Quest-native title from PixelFalconVR, built for players who love the slicing fantasy and want fresh mechanics on top of it.

5. Audio Trip — dance and cardio

Audio Trip is a dance-first rhythm game with a real workout bent: catch gems, ride ribbons, and move your whole body to the music. Less about precision cutting, more about flowing movement — and one of the best cardio payoffs of any game on this list.

6. Smash Drums — rhythm drumming

Smash Drums puts drumsticks in your hands and asks you to smash a full kit to the beat. The most physical rhythm game here — a genuine arm workout — and the closest VR gets to playing real drums.

7. Ragnarock — Viking drumming

Ragnarock is a competitive drumming rhythm game: beat your war drums to power your longship, racing other Viking crews to the finish. The hammer-and-drum feel is completely different from slicing, and the multiplayer races give it a competitive edge the other drumming games do not have.

8. Moon Rider — the free one

Moon Rider is a free, browser-based game in the Beat Saber style — no install, no purchase, playable in any VR headset browser. Simpler than a native Quest title, but for zero cost it is the easiest way to find out whether the slicing genre clicks for you.

9. OhShape — dodge and pose to the beat

OhShape is a rhythm game about your whole body: dodge walls, strike poses, and punch targets in time to the music. It plays more like a workout class than a game, and it is the best rhythm game on Quest if your goal is moving, not just scoring.

How to pick your rhythm game

Want slicing? Beat Saber first, Neon Katana second. Want to dance? Synth Riders or Audio Trip. Want action? Pistol Whip. Want to sweat? OhShape or Smash Drums. Want to spend nothing? Moon Rider. Most rhythm fans end up with two or three of these — the genre is at its best when you rotate between them. What is the best VR rhythm game on Quest?

Beat Saber remains the benchmark — the biggest track library and the most-played rhythm game in VR. Synth Riders is the best dance-first alternative, Pistol Whip is the best rhythm shooter, and Neon Katana is the best katana-slicing entry in the genre.

What are the best rhythm VR games like Beat Saber?

The games most like Beat Saber on Quest are Neon Katana (dual katanas, draw-slash duels, D-to-SSS ranks), Synth Riders (dance rails instead of slicing), and Moon Rider (free, browser-based, Beat Saber style). See our full guide to the best Beat Saber alternatives on Quest.

Is there a free VR rhythm game on Quest?

Yes — Moon Rider is a free browser-based game in the Beat Saber style, playable in any VR headset browser with no install.

What is the best VR rhythm game for a workout?

Audio Trip and OhShape are the best cardio rhythm games on Quest — full-body movement to the music. Smash Drums is the most physical of all: a genuine arm workout on a virtual drum kit.
