Games Like Phasmophobia on Quest (2026)

Phasmophobia is the ghost-hunting game everyone wants on Quest — but it is not on standalone Quest. It runs on PC VR and PS VR2. If you want the same feeling on the headset you already own, these are the ghost hunting VR games that actually run on Meta Quest.

1. Spirit Sweep VR — the ghost-cleaning entry

Spirit Sweep VR is the closest thing to a Phasmophobia-style ghost hunt built natively for standalone Quest. You are a Sweeper in a decayed Victorian estate: scan for hidden spirits with your proximity pulse, stun them, weaken them with your dual-helix exorcism beam, and pull them into your containment vortex. 16 undead spirit types across 3 haunted zones and 10 missions, ending in a Boss Housekeeper finale. It plays differently from Phasmophobia — mission-based action instead of co-op evidence gathering — but the core fantasy is the same: walk into a haunted place, find what is in there, and deal with it.

2. Ghostbusters: Rise of the Ghost Lord — the licensed co-op hunt

The closest game to Phasmophobia in structure: a handheld ghost-trapping device, missions across haunted locations, and co-op play with friends. It is lighter and funnier than Phasmophobia — you are a Ghostbuster, not an investigator — but the loop of tracking, blasting, and trapping ghosts is the same one Phasmophobia players love.

3. The Exorcist: Legion VR — investigation horror

An episodic horror game about investigating haunted rooms and performing exorcisms. It is the closest to Phasmophobia's investigation side on Quest: you examine the room, find what is wrong, and perform the rites. Short chapters, genuinely unnerving in the dark, and the most Phasmophobia-like in tone.

4. Wraith: The Oblivion – Afterlife — atmospheric haunting

You play a wraith haunting the Barclay Mansion, and the horror comes from atmosphere rather than jump scares. Less of a hunt and more of a haunting — but for Phasmophobia players who love the dread of a dark house and a story that unfolds room by room, it scratches the same itch.

5. Propagation: Paradise Hotel — survival horror

A survival horror game in a ruined hotel overrun by the infected. It is more action and survival than investigation, but the feeling — dark corridors, scarce resources, something one room behind you — is the same tension that makes Phasmophobia work.

6. Resident Evil 4 VR — horror, not ghost hunting

Not a ghost game, but worth including: the best horror game on Quest full stop. If what you love about Phasmophobia is the fear of being hunted in the dark, Resident Evil 4 VR delivers it at the highest quality on the platform — a full campaign, rebuilt for first-person VR.

What about Phasmophobia itself?

As of 2026, Phasmophobia has not come to standalone Quest. It runs on PC VR (SteamVR) and PS VR2. If you have a gaming PC, playing it there with a Quest linked over Air Link or cable is the real thing. If you only have the standalone headset, the games above are the honest alternatives — and Spirit Sweep VR is the one built for Quest from the ground up. What is the closest game to Phasmophobia on Quest?

Ghostbusters: Rise of the Ghost Lord is the closest in structure — a handheld ghost-trapping device, co-op play, and missions across haunted locations. Spirit Sweep VR is the closest in spirit for solo players: a mission-based ghost hunt built natively for standalone Quest, with 16 spirit types to track and capture.

Is Spirit Sweep VR like Phasmophobia?

It shares the ghost-hunting premise, but plays differently. Phasmophobia is a co-op investigation game where you gather evidence to identify the ghost, and it runs on PC VR or PS VR2. Spirit Sweep VR is a mission-based action game where you weaken and capture spirits with the Spirit Sweeper, and it is built for standalone Meta Quest VR.

What are the best ghost hunting VR games?

On PC VR, Phasmophobia is the defining ghost-hunting game. On standalone Quest, the best ghost hunting VR games are Spirit Sweep VR, Ghostbusters: Rise of the Ghost Lord, and The Exorcist: Legion VR — all built for the headset with no PC required.
