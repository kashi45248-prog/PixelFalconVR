Best Beat Saber Alternatives on Quest (2026)

Beat Saber is still the benchmark for VR rhythm games — but if you have played it to death, these are the games like Beat Saber on Quest that are actually worth your time. Each one keeps the music-driven flow and adds its own twist.

1. Beat Saber — the game everything else is measured against

Start with the honest truth: there is no replacement for Beat Saber, only companions. Slice blocks to the beat with red and blue sabers, chase high scores, and play the biggest licensed track library in VR. If you only own one rhythm game, this is it. The games below are for when you want more of the feeling with a different flavour.

2. Synth Riders — the dance-first alternative

Synth Riders trades cutting for flowing: ride glowing rails with your hands, catch orbs, and dance through the track. It is the most graceful of the Beat Saber alternatives, with a strong freestyle mode and a workout feel that sneaks up on you. Pick this one if you want rhythm without combat.

3. Pistol Whip — rhythm, but with guns

Pistol Whip keeps the on-rails music flow and swaps blades for bullets. You shoot enemies to the beat while dodging through a cinematic action scene — part rhythm game, part John Wick. It is the best Beat Saber alternative for players who want adrenaline over precision slicing.

4. Neon Katana — the katana-slicing entry

Neon Katana is the closest thing to Beat Saber with a blade in each hand — dual glowing katanas, red and blue target matching, and a D-to-SSS ranking ladder on every track. What makes it different: an iaijutsu draw-slash where your katana rests sheathed until the beat tells you to draw, and parry-and-counter duels against neon ronin between tracks. It is a 2026 Quest-native title from PixelFalconVR, built for players who love the slicing fantasy and want fresh mechanics on top of it.

5. Moon Rider — the free warm-up

Moon Rider is a free, browser-based game in the Beat Saber style — no install, no purchase, playable in any VR headset browser. The visuals and track selection are simpler than a native Quest title, but for zero cost it is the easiest way to find out whether the slicing genre clicks for you.

6. Audio Trip — dance and cardio

Audio Trip is a dance-first rhythm game with a real workout bent: catch gems, ride ribbons, and move your whole body to the music. It is less about precision cutting and more about flowing movement, and it has one of the best cardio payoffs of any game on this list.

7. Smash Drums — rhythm drumming

Smash Drums puts drumsticks in your hands and asks you to smash a full kit to the beat. It is the most physical game here — a genuine arm workout — and the closest VR gets to playing real drums. Great as a second rhythm game alongside a slicer like Beat Saber or Neon Katana.

How to choose

If you want the purest slicing, start with Beat Saber and add Neon Katana for fresh mechanics. If you want to move your whole body, Synth Riders or Audio Trip. If you want action, Pistol Whip. If you want to spend nothing, Moon Rider. Most rhythm-game fans end up owning two or three of these — they scratch different itches, and the genre is at its best when you rotate between them.

What is the best Beat Saber alternative on Quest?

It depends on what you want: Synth Riders is the closest dance-first alternative, Pistol Whip swaps sabers for guns, and Neon Katana is the katana-slicing entry with draw-slash duels and D-to-SSS ranks. Beat Saber itself is still the benchmark — these are the games to play next to it, not instead of it.

Is there a free game like Beat Saber on Quest?

Yes — Moon Rider is a free browser-based game in the Beat Saber style, playable in any VR headset browser with no install. It is a great warm-up before buying a full native Quest rhythm game.

Is Neon Katana like Beat Saber?

Yes — same genre, own identity. Like Beat Saber, you slice targets to the beat with glowing blades and chase high scores. Neon Katana adds dual katanas, an iaijutsu draw-slash mechanic, neon ronin parry-and-counter duels, and D-to-SSS performance ranks across every track.

What is the best VR rhythm game on Quest 3 in 2026?

Beat Saber remains the benchmark, with Synth Riders and Pistol Whip close behind. Neon Katana (2026) is the newest katana-slicing VR rhythm game built natively for Quest 2 and Quest 3 — worth a look if you want fresh tracks and mechanics in the slicing genre.
