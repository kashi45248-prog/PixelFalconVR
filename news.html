Neon Katana launch sale: 50% off, Oct 26 – Nov 8

24 Oct 2026

To celebrate launch week, Neon Katana — our VR rhythm game for Meta Quest — is 50% off on the Meta Horizon Store from October 26 through November 8. The sale covers the full game: dual-katana slicing, iaijutsu draw-slashes, neon ronin parry-and-counter duels, and the D-to-SSS ranking ladder on every track.

Neon Katana is live on the Meta Horizon Store

23 Oct 2026

PixelFalconVR's debut title is out now for Meta Quest 2 and Quest 3. Neon Katana is a VR rhythm game built around the katana-slicing fantasy: match red and blue targets to your blades, chain combos to the beat, and climb from D to SSS rank — then face neon ronin in parry-and-counter duels between tracks.
