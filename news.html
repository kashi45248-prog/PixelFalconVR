Dev note: how the draw-slash mechanic works

26 Oct 2026

Neon Katana's signature mechanic started from a simple idea: your katana rests sheathed until the beat tells you to draw. One clean motion — draw, cut, resheathe — instead of constant swinging. It changes how a rhythm track feels to play: the quiet beats matter as much as the loud ones, because the draw itself is timed to the music. The full game is on the Neon Katana page, with the trailer, the core loop, and the D-to-SSS ranking ladder.
