Best VR Horror Games on Meta Quest (2026)

Horror hits differently in VR — there is no screen between you and the thing in the dark. These are the best VR horror games on Meta Quest in 2026, from full-length campaigns to ghost-hunting missions.

1. Resident Evil 4 VR — the benchmark

The full Resident Evil 4 campaign rebuilt for VR, and still the most complete horror game on Quest. First-person aiming, physical reloads, and a village full of things that want you dead. If you only play one horror game on the headset, play this one.

2. Propagation: Paradise Hotel — survival dread

A survival horror game set in a ruined hotel overrun by the infected. Tight corridors, scarce ammo, and a constant feeling that something is one room behind you. The scariest pure survival horror on standalone Quest.

3. Wraith: The Oblivion – Afterlife — slow, atmospheric dread

You play a wraith haunting the Barclay Mansion, and the horror comes from atmosphere rather than jump scares: whispered voices, shifting corridors, and a story that gets under your skin. The best choice if you want horror that lingers after you take the headset off.

4. The Exorcist: Legion VR — episodic exorcism horror

An episodic horror game built around exorcisms: investigate haunted rooms, find what is possessing them, and perform the rites. Short, sharp chapters that are easy to play one at a time — and genuinely unnerving in the dark.

5. Five Nights at Freddy's: Help Wanted — animatronic dread

The FNAF formula in VR: survive the night against animatronics that move when you are not looking. Being inside the office instead of watching it on a monitor makes the whole thing considerably worse — in the best way.

6. Metro Awakening — story-driven horror

A narrative horror game set in the Metro universe, with a proper story, voice acting, and the series' signature oppressive tunnels. The most cinematic horror on this list, and the newest big-budget entry.

7. Spirit Sweep VR — the ghost-cleaning entry

Spirit Sweep VR is the ghost-hunting entry on this list: a first-person ghost-cleaning campaign for standalone Quest. Wield the Spirit Sweeper — scan, stun, weaken, and contain 16 undead spirit types across 3 haunted zones and 10 missions, ending in a Boss Housekeeper finale. It is horror with a job to do: the scares come from the hunt, the darkness waves, and the things closing in while your barrier holds. If you like Phasmophobia or Ghostbusters VR, this is the Quest-native version of that fantasy.

8. Ghostbusters: Rise of the Ghost Lord — lighter ghost-busting

The licensed, lighter take on ghost hunting: trap ghosts with a proton pack across San Francisco, solo or in co-op. Less scary than the rest of this list, more fun — a good palate cleanser after the heavier entries, and the closest thing to Phasmophobia's co-op ghost-hunting loop on Quest.

9. The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners — zombie survival horror

A full survival-horror campaign in a flooded New Orleans: scavenge, craft, and fight walkers up close with melee weapons that feel weighty in your hands. More survival than jump-scare, and one of the deepest games on Quest full stop.
